HOW TO PLAY

▶ Fly to a planet, INVADE. Draft units with 20 tokens. Destroy all enemy cores.
▶ Dock at the center Station: research & buy units (tech tree). Kills + conquests give 🔬 RP. Conquered planets pay CR/min.
▶ Mouse: drag-select, right-click move/attack. Q all, X stop, Ctrl+1-5 groups, WASD pan.
▶ Touch: tap unit, tap ground/enemy. ALL, BOX, drag to pan.
▶ Fog: you only see what your units see. Air sees farther. Firing reveals a unit.
