CONTROLS

DESKTOP
WASD / Arrows: fly   Mouse: aim   Click / hold: fire
E or Space: enter planet / dock / launch
M: galaxy map   Esc / P: pause

TOUCH
Left thumb: fly   Right thumb: aim + fire
Tap the yellow button to enter / launch

GOAL
Land on planets and destroy every enemy CORE to conquer it. Hover over the landing pad to repair and launch back to space. Dock at the Station (center of the galaxy) to buy ships.
